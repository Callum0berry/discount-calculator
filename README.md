## Discount Calculation Guide

This application helps users calculate
discounted product prices.

### Calculation Inputs

- Original product price
- Discount percentage

### Calculation Results

- Discount amount
- Final price
- Total savings

### How to Use

1. Enter the original price.
2. Enter the discount percentage.
3. Calculate the discounted price.
4. Review the savings.

### Validation

Ensure that prices are positive
and discount percentages are valid.
## Manual Testing Checklist

- [ ] Calculate a ten-percent discount on a known price.
- [ ] Verify the savings and final price add to the original.
- [ ] Test zero-percent and full-price discounts.
